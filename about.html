THE OFFICIAL BIOGRAPHY

ERIC "TRiSTAR" MCKINNEY

West Coast Royalty • Founder of PSG4L LLC • PSG4L Presents Architect

Known to legions of hip-hop devotees as TRiSTAR (and across social media as @westsiidetrizzy), Eric McKinney stands as an enduring pillar of authentic West Coast hip-hop, soulful musicality, and lyrical excellence.

Emerging from the vibrant Los Angeles hip-hop scene, TRiSTAR forged a legacy of collaboration with West Coast titans. He established his sharp delivery and undeniable charisma through legendary appearances on Kurupt's landmark projects, including Digital Smoke (with J. Wells), Streetlights, and as a core member of the acclaimed group Anterazh on the album Originals alongside Slo Stallone and T. McKinney.

In 2025 and 2026, TRiSTAR elevates his artistic mastery to unprecedented heights with the release of BLESSINGS (Single). Executive produced by HOLLYWOOD and composed alongside longtime musical collaborator Cary “Chase” Johnson, the project represents the ultimate marriage of hard-hitting street grit and lush, melodic soul.

CHRONOLOGY

CAREER MILESTONES

Key eras in TRiSTAR's musical journey.

2007 – 2011

The Anterazh & Kurupt Era

Collaborations with Kurupt (Tha Dogg Pound) on Digital Smoke, Originals with Anterazh, and Streetlights. Release of underground favorite Lokopedia (2011).

2018 – 2024

PSG4L LLC & Renaissance Man

Establishment of PSG4L LLC imprint, hit single "Act A Fool" (2018), "Rah Rah" ft. Royal skye (2021), and the acclaimed solo album Renaissance Man (2024).

2025 – 2026

BLESSING$ & PSG4L Movement

Release of PSG4L vol.1 RIP SLO tribute and the official premiere of BLESSINGS (Single), produced by HOLLYWOOD.
